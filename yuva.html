About YUVA

YUVA is an initiative of Chavara Youth, a Catholic organisation under the aegis of the Jayantian Catholic Association of Kristu Jayanti Deemed to be University, Bengaluru, united in the youthfulness of Christ in prayer and action.

YUVA stands for Youth United for Visionary Actions. Inspired by the visionary legacy of St. Kuriakose Elias Chavara, it invites Catholic youth to envision and embrace the mission of the Church through sharing the Gospel and celebrating the Word of God.

As a global fest of literary and cultural competitions, YUVA brings young people together to express their creativity, celebrate their faith, build unity, and grow through meaningful cultural exchange.

Before you take part

General Guidelines

These guidelines are applicable to all participants attending YUVA UNVEILED 2027. All participants are expected to follow the rules and instructions provided by the organisers.

Download the brochure
Who can participate?
  • Expected age group: 17-25 years
  • Participants must carry a valid college ID or approved identification.
  • Bonafide certificate from parish or college is accepted.
Languages, originality, and submission format
  • English shall be the official language of the event.
  • Participants must follow the specific requirements of their respective competitions.
  • All performances and submissions must follow the competition guidelines.
Rules, discipline, and participation
  • Participants must report on time and follow the event schedule.
  • Attendance at the inauguration, all sessions and valedictory function is compulsory.
  • Participants must maintain discipline and respectful behaviour.
  • Day 1 and Day 2 competitions take place simultaneously.
  • Participants cannot register for two competitions scheduled at the same time.
  • Prior permission is required before leaving the campus
  • Alcohol, cigarettes and narcotic substances are strictly prohibited.
Judges, results, and disqualification
  • Competition marks and individual scores will not be disclosed.
  • Misconduct or violation of rules may lead to disqualification.
  • Participants must not approach judges directly.
  • All queries and grievances must be addressed to event convenors.
  • Judges' and organisers' decisions are final and binding.
Registration and important dates
  • Event dates: 25-26 January 2027.
  • Registration deadline: 31 December 2026.
  • Venue: Kristu Jayanti Deemed to be University, Bengaluru.
