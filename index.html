Welcome to
Chavara Youth

"I encourage you young people, always to be living stones in the Church, to build up the Church, by being united to Jesus". — Pope Francis

Chavara Youth is a vibrant community of young individuals from Kristu Jayanti Deemed to be University, Bengaluru. We're inspired by the teachings of St. Kuriakose Elias Chavara and united in the youthfulness of Christ. Our mission is to nurture spiritual growth, celebrate the exuberance of youth, and connect with young hearts through action enriched by prayer.

Our Mission

Chavara Youth is on a mission to sculpt the youth for holistic development that aligns with the values and principles of the Church.

Our Vision

Empowering youth to embrace faith and celebrate youthfulness, deeply rooted in the love of Christ and the teachings of the Church.
